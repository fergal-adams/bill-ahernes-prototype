Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a general-purpose privacy policy for Bill Aherne's, a pub at 22 Aston Quay, Temple Bar, Dublin 2, D02 FH28. It explains, in plain terms, what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the pub and respond to enquiries. This may include:

  • Contact details you give us directly, such as your name, email address or phone number, if you get in touch about a booking, private hire, or general enquiry
  • Basic, non-identifying analytics about how visitors use this website, such as which pages are viewed

How we use it

We use your information to respond to enquiries, manage bookings or hires, and understand how our website is used so we can improve it. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Sharing

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us run the website or handle enquiries (for example, email or hosting providers), and only to the extent needed for them to provide that service.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by getting in touch using the details below.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please get in touch via the contact details on our homepage.
