Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a general-purpose cookie policy for Bill Aherne's website. It explains, in plain terms, how cookies and similar technologies may be used on this site.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work properly and can also be used to understand how it's used.

Cookies we may use

  • Essential cookies — needed for the website to function correctly. These can't be switched off.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are popular, so we can improve it. These are anonymised where possible.

We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

Managing cookies

Most browsers let you control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting them. Turning off non-essential cookies won't stop you using the site, but may affect how some features work.
