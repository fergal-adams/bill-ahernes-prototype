From Bill to Liam to Willie,
a real family story.

Bill Aherne came to Dublin from Tipperary in the 1930s to serve his time in the publican trade. In 1946 he took on the Palace Bar, one of Dublin's great literary pubs, which has stayed in the family ever since. Bill's son Liam took over in 1978, and his grandson Willie runs it today. Three generation under one pub roof.

In 2026, Willie set out to honour his grandfather's legacy by opening a pub in his name. He found the perfect spot along the River Liffey, ten seconds from O'Connell Bridge, in a building that's been a Dublin pub since the 1880s. It's run the way he believes his grandfather would have wanted it… traditional, with care, craft and a real love of craic.
